# SIP10 - FINAL PROJECT REQUIREMENTS
**Agentic AI + AI Governance + AI Orchestration**

---

## Project Title
**Building a Simple Governed Agentic AI System**

## Project Objective
Create a simple Agentic AI system that can perform a specific task or solve a problem. You are free to choose what type of Agentic AI you want to create.

Your system must demonstrate:
$$\text{PLAN} \longrightarrow \text{ORCHESTRATE} \longrightarrow \text{EXECUTE} \longrightarrow \text{CHECK} \longrightarrow \text{HUMAN APPROVAL} \longrightarrow \text{OUTPUT}$$

Your project must also apply the basic concepts of the **NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF)**:
$$\text{GOVERN} \longrightarrow \text{MAP} \longrightarrow \text{MEASURE} \longrightarrow \text{MANAGE}$$

---

## Basic Workflow

```
[USER] 
  │
  ▼
[PLANNER] ──► [ORCHESTRATOR] ──► [SKILL/AGENT] ──► [SKILL/AGENT] ──► [REVIEWER] ──► [HUMAN APPROVAL] ──► [FINAL OUTPUT]
```

---

## Required Components

### 1. AI Orchestration
Your agents and skills must work together. The Orchestrator controls the workflow and determines which agent or skill should work next.

### 2. AI Agents
Your system must have at least **3 agents or AI roles**. The roles depend on your project.

#### Example Agent Breakdown:
* **Agent 1 – Planner:** Understands the user's request and creates a plan.
* **Agent 2 – Executor:** Performs the main task.
* **Agent 3 – Reviewer:** Checks the work produced by the AI.

*(You may give your agents different names depending on your project.)*

* **AI Travel Assistant:** Travel Planner $\rightarrow$ Travel Researcher $\rightarrow$ Travel Reviewer
* **AI Marketing Assistant:** Marketing Planner $\rightarrow$ Marketing Analyst $\rightarrow$ Marketing Reviewer

## Required Deliverables

Submit the following:

1. **Agentic AI Prototype** (A working or demonstrable AI system.)
2. **Project Description** (Explain what your AI does and what problem it solves.)
3. **AI Agents** (At least 3 agents/roles.)
4. **SKILL.md Files** (At least 3 skills.)
5. **Orchestration Workflow** (Show how your agents and skills work together.)
6. **System Diagram** (Show the complete AI workflow.)
7. **NIST AI RMF Worksheet** (Complete: GOVERN, MAP, MEASURE, MANAGE)
8. **Risk Assessment** (At least 5 identified risks.)
9. **Testing** (At least 5 test cases.)
10. **AI Governance Rules** (At least 5 rules.)
11. **Human Approval** (Show where and why human approval is required.)
12. **Final Presentation** (Maximum 15 minutes.)

## Final Project Formula

$$\text{PLAN} \longrightarrow \text{ORCHESTRATE} \longrightarrow \text{EXECUTE} \longrightarrow \text{CHECK} \longrightarrow \text{GOVERN} \longrightarrow \text{HUMAN APPROVAL} \longrightarrow \text{OUTPUT}$$

> **Note:** The goal is not to build the most complicated AI system. The goal is to demonstrate that you understand how to create a simple Agentic AI system that can perform a task, work with other AI components, follow governance rules, manage risks, and maintain human oversight.
